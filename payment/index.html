Paying in China as a foreign visitor

Short answer: link your foreign Visa/Mastercard/UnionPay to Alipay or WeChat Pay, then pay by scanning merchant QR codes. That covers the overwhelming majority of places. Bring one backup card and a little cash.

The three practical routes

Networks differ by app. Alipay supports Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club and Discover; WeChat Pay supports Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club and JCB. Many Alipay+ partner wallets (AlipayHK, Kakao Pay, Touch ’n Go, TrueMoney, Changi Pay, NAVER Pay, Toss Pay and others) can also scan Chinese merchant QR codes directly.

Other ways to pay without a Chinese card

  • Tap-to-pay transit: several city metros accept contactless international cards at fare gates (for example Shenzhen Metro across its lines, covering UnionPay, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and JCB).
  • Alipay TourCard: an in-app travel card you top up in your home currency and spend by QR code.
  • Cash: still accepted widely; useful as a fallback when a QR payment fails.

Setup steps (do this before you land)

  1. Install Alipay or WeChat Pay and register with your phone number.
  2. Open the card-binding page and enter your foreign card details.
  3. Complete any verification your card issuer requires (SMS / app confirmation).
  4. Test with a small top-up or a low-value payment.
  5. Set the app’s payment method to the bound card, then scan merchant QR codes.

Limits and gotchas

  • Foreign-card transactions have per-transaction and cumulative caps that differ by card type and change over time.
  • Some merchants (especially hotels) may place a pre-authorisation hold; keep headroom.
  • If a scan fails, ask for the merchant’s printed QR and try again, or fall back to cash.
  • Keep your home-country SIM active for SMS verification during setup.

FAQ

Can I pay with my foreign Visa or Mastercard in China?

Yes, in two ways. You can link Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club, Discover, American Express or UnionPay to Alipay or WeChat Pay and pay by scanning merchant QR codes. Overseas wallets (including some non-Chinese ones) can also scan directly at supported merchants, though support varies.

Do I need a Chinese bank account?

No. Linking an international card to Alipay/WeChat Pay is enough for most spending, including taxis, hotels, restaurants and shops that display a QR code.

What are the limits on foreign cards?

Foreign-card payments through Alipay/WeChat Pay have per-transaction and cumulative limits. The exact figures change; check your app’s foreign-card page before travelling and carry a backup card plus some cash.

Is cash still accepted?

Cash is accepted almost everywhere, but small vendors increasingly prefer mobile payment. Carry a modest amount of RMB as a fallback.
