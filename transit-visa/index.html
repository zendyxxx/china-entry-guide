China 240-hour visa-free transit

Short answer: if you are a national of one of 57 countries, hold a confirmed dated-and-seated connecting ticket, and travel from point A through China to a third country or region, you can enter visa-free through one of 65 designated ports and stay up to 240 hours (10 days). Your final destination must differ from your origin.

Common mistakes

  • Origin = destination. Flying New York → Beijing → New York is a return, not a transit; the policy requires a third country/region.
  • Two separate tickets. A single through-ticket is expected; self-transfer on unrelated bookings can fail.
  • Wrong port. Entering through a non-designated port invalidates the exemption.
  • Overstaying 240 hours. Overstay leads to fines, detention and future entry bans.

Documents you must present

  • Passport (or other international travel document) valid for at least 3 months.
  • A confirmed, dated and seated connecting ticket to a third country/region within 240 hours. Without it, the temporary entry procedure cannot be completed.
  • The temporary-entry arrival card (临时入境外国人入境卡), filled in and checked.

When the border officer will refuse

  • Passport valid for less than 3 months, or bearing a Chinese visa-refusal stamp.
  • Illegal entry, residence or employment record in China within the past 5 years.
  • No onward ticket, or origin = destination.
  • Any other circumstance under Chinese law that bars entry.

Permitted activities are tourism, business, visits and family calls. Work, study and journalism require a visa obtained before travel. Overstaying, or staying outside the permitted area without a stay permit, brings fines and penalties.
