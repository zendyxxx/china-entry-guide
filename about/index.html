About China Entry Guide

China Entry Guide is an independent, English-language reference for visitors planning a trip to mainland China. We focus on the things that decide whether a trip starts smoothly: entry rules, payments, connectivity, rail and timing.

How we work

  • Primary sources first. Policy pages cite the National Immigration Administration, embassies and consulates, and official payment providers.
  • Dated pages. Every page shows its last-verified date so you can judge freshness.
  • Structured content. Answers first, tables over prose, and schema markup throughout — built to be read by humans and cited by AI assistants accurately.
  • Local tools. Our checkers run in your browser. We never collect your passport number.

How we make money

Some pages contain affiliate links to booking platforms and travel services. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never determine what we say about a rule or a route. See our privacy and terms.

Contact

Corrections and questions: hello@chinaentryguide.com. We publish corrections within 48 hours of verification.

Editorial independence

We are not a government agency and not a visa agent. The final decision on entry always rests with the border inspector.
