Hainan 30-day visa-free entry

Short answer: nationals of 61 countries can enter through a Hainan port and stay up to 30 days, but only within Hainan province. It suits beach, wellness and family trips; it is not a gateway to the rest of mainland China.

Conditions

  • Ordinary passport of a listed country.
  • Enter through an open port in Hainan (Haikou, Sanya, Qionghai, etc.).
  • Remain within Hainan province for the whole stay.
  • Up to 30 days; tourism, business, visits and family purposes.

The 61 eligible countries

Europe 40 · Americas 6 · Oceania 2 · Asia 13 (includes Malaysia, Thailand, Kazakhstan, Philippines).
