China high-speed rail: a practical guide

Short answer: high-speed rail is the easiest way to move between major cities. Book with your passport, arrive 45–60 minutes early, and validate your ticket at the gate. Trains are frequent, punctual and comfortable.

Booking channels

Ways to get a ticket
ChannelNotes
Station counters / machinesBring your passport. Staff at the "foreign passenger" or service counter can help.
12306 (official)The authoritative source. App/website English support is limited; you will need your passport details.
Third-party platformsEnglish interfaces and support; a service fee usually applies. Choose established platforms.

Main corridors for first-time visitors

  • Beijing ↔ Shanghai — about 4.5–6 hours; the backbone route.
  • Shanghai ↔ Hangzhou ↔ Suzhou — short hops, ideal for a city cluster.
  • Beijing ↔ Xi’an — reaches the Terracotta Army region.
  • Guangzhou ↔ Shenzhen ↔ Hong Kong — links the Greater Bay Area.
  • Chengdu ↔ Chongqing — Sichuan Basin pair, food and pandas.

At the station

  1. Find the departures board (pinyin city names match ticket codes like BJP / SHH).
  2. Go through security with your luggage.
  3. Scan your passport/ticket at the gate; check the platform number.
  4. Board by carriage number shown on the platform floor markings.

FAQ

Do I need to book China trains in advance?

For peak periods (Golden Week, Spring Festival, summer) yes — popular high-speed routes sell out. Off-peak, you can usually buy same-day tickets, but booking ahead saves stress.

Can foreigners buy train tickets with a foreign passport?

Yes. Foreign passengers book with a passport. Tickets can be bought online via 12306 or third-party platforms, or at station counters with your passport.

Is the 12306 app available in English?

The official 12306 app has limited English; many travellers use a reputable third-party booking platform with English support, or buy at stations.

How early should I arrive at the station?

At least 45–60 minutes before departure for high-speed rail: security and passport checks take time.
