Best time to visit China

Short answer: April–May and September–October are the sweet spots for most first-time itineraries — mild weather, clearer skies and fewer domestic crowds than summer and Golden Week. Hainan is the exception: its best window is November–March.

Month-by-month snapshot

SpringApr–MayMild, pleasantPopular; book ahead for Labour Day (1–5 May)
SummerJun–AugHot, humid, rainy southPeak domestic travel; typhoon risk Jul–Sep
AutumnSep–OctCool, clearExcellent; National Day (1–7 Oct) is very crowded
WinterNov–MarCold north, mild southFewest crowds; Harbin ice festival Jan–Feb; Hainan high season

By region

  • Beijing / Xi’an / north — spring and autumn; winters are cold and dry.
  • Shanghai / Hangzhou / Suzhou — spring and autumn; June brings the plum-rain season.
  • Guilin / Yangshuo / Guizhou — April–October; lush landscapes after the rains.
  • Sichuan / Yunnan — spring and autumn; altitude means cool evenings year-round.
  • Hainan — November to March; summer is hot and typhoon-prone.

Holiday effect: Chinese public holidays concentrate domestic travel massively. Prices rise and transport sells out around 1–5 May, 1–7 October and late January–February (Spring Festival).
