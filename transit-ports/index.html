240-hour transit: open ports and permitted areas

Short answer: the 240-hour visa-free transit applies at 65 open ports in 24 provinces, regions and municipalities. You may enter and exit through any of them and travel across those regions. Any other port does not qualify.

Region coverage changes with each policy round. Treat this list as our last-verified snapshot (2026-11-26); if a specific city matters to you, verify it against the NIA announcement first.

Entry and exit must match

  • You may enter and exit through any qualifying port — they do not have to be the same one.
  • The onward ticket must leave for a third country/region different from where you started.
  • Stay starts counting at 00:00 the day after entry, not at landing time.
