Shanghai for first-time visitors

Two to three days along the Huangpu: Bund, Yu Garden, French Concession, one museum.

2–3 dayssuggested minimum
Mar–Mayor Sep–Nov, best weather

Short answer: two to three days along the Huangpu river line — Bund, Yu Garden, French Concession, one museum — works well for a first trip. Arrive into Pudong (PVG) or Hongqiao (SHA); the metro reaches central areas quickly.

A two-to-three-day structure

Suggested structure
DayAreaNotes
1The Bund + Nanjing RoadWalk the Bund at dusk for the skyline; mornings are quieter.
2Yu Garden + old town + French ConcessionCombine the old town lanes with a tree-lined afternoon walk.
3Museum or a water-town day tripPick either a city museum or a nearby water town, not both.

Getting there

  • By air: Pudong (PVG) is the main international gateway; Hongqiao (SHA) is closer to the city and handles many domestic hops.
  • By high-speed rail: Shanghai Hongqiao is a major hub — see our rail guide.
  • Transit policy: Shanghai is a qualifying port under the 240-hour scheme — test your routing.

Practical notes

  • The metro is clean and English-signed; scan with Alipay or WeChat Pay where supported.
  • Walkable neighbourhoods reward slower exploration — leave room for unplanned stops.
  • Typhoon season affects summer and early autumn plans; check season advice.
