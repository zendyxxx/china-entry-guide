Xi’an for first-time visitors

Two days: the Terracotta Army, then the walled old city and Muslim Quarter.

2 dayssuggested minimum
Apr–Junor Sep–Oct, best weather
XIYXi’an Xianyang International
Xi’an Northhigh-speed rail station

Short answer: two days — Terracotta Army on day one, the walled old city and Muslim Quarter on day two. Arrive by high-speed rail from Beijing or Shanghai, or fly into Xi’an Xianyang (XIY).

Two-day structure

Suggested structure
DayAreaNotes
1Terracotta ArmyTravel early; allow several hours for the pits and museum.
2City wall + museums + Muslim QuarterRent a bike on the wall if the weather is kind; end with the food streets.

Getting there

  • High-speed rail: direct links from Beijing and Shanghai make Xi’an an easy third stop — see our rail guide.
  • By air: Xi’an Xianyang International (XIY) serves the region.
  • Entry status: Xi’an is within the permitted region for the 240-hour transit policy — confirm your port and permitted area.

Practical notes

  • The walled centre is compact; cycling or walking beats driving inside the walls.
  • The Muslim Quarter is busiest in the evening — plan dinner there.
  • Combine Xi’an with Beijing and Shanghai using our 7-day route.
