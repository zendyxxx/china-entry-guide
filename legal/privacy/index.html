Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-26.

What we do not collect

Our visa-free and transit checkers run entirely in your browser. The country you select, the itinerary you type and your checklist ticks never leave your device. We do not ask for passport numbers, dates of birth or any identity document.

What we may collect

  • Standard server logs. Hosting providers typically log IP addresses, timestamps and requested pages for security and debugging. These are retained for a limited period.
  • Aggregated analytics. If analytics is enabled, we use privacy-respecting, aggregate metrics (page views, referrers). No advertising profile is built.
  • Affiliate partners. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, the partner sets its own cookies and collects its own data under its own policy.

Cookies

This site itself sets no marketing cookies. Checklist progress is stored in your browser’s local storage and never transmitted.

Your choices

You can clear local storage at any time to erase your checklist state. To opt out of analytics, use your browser’s tracking protection.
