Getting online in China

Short answer: buy a travel eSIM that covers mainland China, activate it before boarding, and install WeChat, Amap (Gaode) and a maps/translation app in advance. Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and X are blocked in mainland China, so plan alternatives before you land.

Apps to install before you fly

  • WeChat (微信) — messaging, mini-programs, payments. Non-negotiable.
  • Amap (高德地图) or Apple Maps — navigation; Google Maps does not work reliably.
  • Alipay / WeChat Pay — see our payment guide.
  • Baidu Translate / a translation app — offline packs for signs and menus.
  • Didi (滴滴) — ride-hailing; can be used with a foreign card.

FAQ

Which eSIM works for China?

Look for travel eSIMs that explicitly list mainland China as covered, support local carriers (China Mobile / China Unicom / China Telecom), and activate before departure. Verify coverage maps and fair-use policies before buying.

Is Google blocked in mainland China?

Yes. Google services, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and X are blocked in mainland China. Messaging works via WeChat; navigation works via Amap (Gaode) or Apple Maps.

Should I use a VPN instead of an eSIM?

A legal local SIM/eSIM plus local apps covers most needs. VPN use in China is legally restricted; we do not advise or instruct on circumvention tools.

Can I keep my home number?

Yes — many eSIM plans run alongside your home SIM, so you still receive SMS and calls on your original number.
