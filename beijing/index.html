Beijing for first-time visitors

Three days: the Forbidden City, one Great Wall section, hutongs and parks.

3 dayssuggested minimum
Apr–Mayor Sep–Oct, best weather
PKX · PEKtwo international airports
Fengtai · Chaoyangmain high-speed rail stations

Short answer: plan three full days, book the Forbidden City and your Great Wall section ahead, and use the metro plus DiDi for everything else. Arrive by air into Daxing (PKX) or Capital (PEK), or by high-speed rail into Beijing Fengtai / Beijing Chaoyang.

A three-day core circuit

Suggested structure (adjust to opening days)
DayAreaNotes
1Forbidden City + Jingshan + QianmenTimed entry; enter from the south, exit north toward Jingshan for the view.
2Great Wall (Mutianyu or Badaling)Leave early; bring water and layers. Return in the afternoon for a hutong walk.
3Temple of Heaven + Summer Palace or museumsPick one large sight rather than rushing both.

Getting there

  • By air: Daxing International (PKX) and Capital International (PEK) both serve international routes; express rail and metro connect them to the city.
  • By high-speed rail: Beijing Fengtai and Beijing Chaoyang handle many intercity trains — see our rail guide.
  • Under the 240-hour transit policy: Beijing is a qualifying port; check the open-port list and test your itinerary.

Practical notes

  • Use Amap for navigation and DiDi for rides; both accept linked foreign cards.
  • Carry your passport — some sites and all hotel check-ins require it.
  • Air quality and weather vary sharply by season; check the best-time guide.
