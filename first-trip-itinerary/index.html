First trip to China: 7 days, three cities

Assumptions: you enter with a qualifying passport (check here), you will pay by linked foreign card plus cash, and you travel outside the 1–7 October and late-January holiday peaks. Adjust days freely — this is a framework, not a tour.

Day-by-day framework
DaysBaseFocus
1–3BeijingForbidden City, Great Wall section at Mutianyu or Badaling, hutong walk, temple and park circuit. Arrive early to beat queues; book major sites ahead.
4Rail Beijing → Xi’anHigh-speed train (several hours). Evening: Muslim Quarter and city wall.
5Xi’anTerracotta Army in the morning; afternoons for the museum and old town.
6Rail Xi’an → ShanghaiHigh-speed leg; evening Bund and Nanjing Road area.
7ShanghaiFrench Concession or Yu Garden, museum of your choice, then onward departure.

Which entry path fits this route

  • 30-day single-sided visa-free (if your passport qualifies): simplest, enter and exit through any open port.
  • 240-hour transit: only if your international routing passes from origin through China to a different third country/region, with a confirmed connecting ticket. See transit rules and open ports.
  • Hainan only: this route is not Hainan-based; that scheme confines you to Hainan province.

Practical notes

  • Intercity legs are best done on high-speed rail — see our rail guide.
  • Install WeChat, Amap, Alipay/WeChat Pay and a translation app before departure (eSIM guide).
  • Carry your passport for train travel and hotel check-in.
  • Temple and museum opening days vary; check same-week schedules rather than assuming daily access.
